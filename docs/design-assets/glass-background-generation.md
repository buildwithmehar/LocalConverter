## Desktop prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production website hero BACKGROUND IMAGE only for Local Converter, a private file conversion toolbox. Generate a high-end understated cinematic 3D abstract material composition in a very wide landscape ratio about 3:1 (ideally 3072x1024). A seamless dark charcoal graphite backdrop (#232326 to #27262b), matte and smooth with extremely fine film grain. On the OUTERMOST left edge, a beautifully sculptural flowing folded ribbon made of frosted smoked silver glass, thick rounded smooth edges and soft studio light; part of the sculpture cropped outside the frame. On the OUTERMOST right edge, a complementary folded sheet / layered translucent glass form with a small warm muted terracotta red rim light, softly defocused toward the background. Forms occupy only the outermost 18 percent of each side and a little of the lower corners. The large central 60 percent of the image MUST remain clean, mostly uniform dark charcoal negative space for a white heading and subtitle, with absolutely no objects or bright lines behind the heading. Top central area also dark and clear for an existing navigation bar. Composition feels calm, intentional and expensive, refined physical materials, subtle natural reflections, gentle depth of field, light shadows, not glossy neon, no purple or blue neon. Restrained contrast so it functions as a background rather than an attention-seeking illustration. Frame should fade naturally to dark charcoal at top and bottom edges so it blends with the website. No text, no letters, no logos, no UI, no icons, no file labels, no scattered small objects, no connector lines, no wireframes, no stars, no particles, no watermark. This is a completely new abstract art image, not a screenshot or mockup.
